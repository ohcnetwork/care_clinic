CARE · Phone and tablet setup

Connect your phone or tablet

Do this once on each device. For a Windows computer or Mac, use CARE Clinic and choose Connect to a clinic.

Connect to the clinic Wi-Fi. Only install this certificate if your clinic administrator gave you this address.

On your phone or tablet, open:
http://example.local/setup

1. Download the clinic certificate

This lets your device recognise the clinic's CARE. Use Safari on iPhone or iPad, or Chrome on Android.

Download certificate

On iPhone or iPad, tap Allow when asked to download a profile. Downloading alone does not finish setup.

2. Allow it in Settings

Choose your device below. Keep this page open so you can return to it.

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded. If it is missing, look under General → VPN & Device Management.
  2. Open CARE Clinic Local CA. Tap Install, enter your device passcode if asked, then confirm Install and tap Done.
  3. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
  4. Under Enable Full Trust for Root Certificates, turn on CARE Clinic Local CA. Tap Continue.

Both parts are needed: install the profile and turn on full trust. Then return to Safari.

Android phone / tablet
  1. Open Settings and search for CA certificate. Or go to Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate.
  2. Android shows a security warning. Only continue for your clinic's certificate. Tap Install anyway and enter your device PIN, pattern or password if asked.
  3. Choose Downloads, then root.crt. If asked for a name, enter CARE clinic and confirm.

On some phones, look under Security & privacy → More security settings. Return to Chrome when finished.

3. Open CARE

After completing the Settings steps, open CARE. If the sign-in page opens without a security warning, setup is complete.

Open CARE (new tab)

Still seeing a warning? Close and reopen your browser. Do not bypass the warning to enter patient information.

Having trouble?

Cannot find the file? Check your browser's Downloads list or the device's Downloads folder for root.crt. On iPhone or iPad, download it in Safari.

Installation blocked? Use your device passcode, not your CARE password. For an organisation-managed device or an unknown passcode, ask your clinic administrator. Do not disable security protections.

CARE does not open? Check the clinic Wi-Fi and that the main clinic computer is running. If the address or security warning still causes problems, ask your clinic administrator.

Why is this needed?

Your clinic runs CARE on its own computer. Installing its root certificate changes who your device trusts for secure connections. Only install it from your clinic's trusted setup page.

The browser cannot approve these settings for you. You must complete them once on each phone or tablet.
